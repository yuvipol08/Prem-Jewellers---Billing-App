1. Opening the software

Double-click the Prem Jewellers Billing icon on the desktop. The software opens straight onto the Billing screen with a new invoice number already prepared.

The software works without internet Every bill is saved on this computer. If the internet is off, the top-right corner shows Offline — billing continues. You can keep billing, printing and saving exactly as normal. Internet is only needed for cloud backup and for sending a bill on WhatsApp.

The five screens

ScreenWhat it is for
BillingMaking a new bill. This is where you spend the day.
CustomersThe customer book, with each customer's past purchases.
InvoicesEvery bill ever made. Search, reprint, download.
DashboardToday's sales, this month, this year.
Backup & SettingsShop details, backup, and the emergency button.

Move between screens by clicking the tabs at the top, or by holding Ctrl and pressing 1 to 5.

2. Making a bill

1
Customer. Start typing the customer's name, mobile number or GST number in the Name box. If they have bought from you before, their details appear in a list — use the arrow keys and press Enter to pick them. Everything else fills in by itself.
For a new customer, simply type their details. They are saved automatically when you save the bill — there is nothing extra to do.
2
Items. Fill the item table exactly as you would write the paper bill:
ColumnWhat to enter
HSN7113 for gold and silver jewellery (already filled in)
ParticularsDescription, e.g. Gold Necklace 22K
Gross Wt (g)Total weight including stones
Net Wt (g)Metal weight you are charging for
Rate (₹/g)Today's rate per gram
MakingMaking charge — see below
3
Making charge. Next to the making charge box there is a small selector with three choices. Pick whichever matches how you quoted the customer:
  • ₹ — a flat amount for the item, e.g. ₹1,500
  • /g — an amount per gram, e.g. ₹450 per gram
  • % — a percentage of the metal value, e.g. 12%
4
Check the total. The right side updates as you type — taxable value, CGST, SGST, round off and the grand total, with the amount written out in words. GST is worked out automatically at 3%.
5
Payment. Click Cash, Cheque or Online. For cheque or online, a box appears for the cheque number or transaction reference. If the customer is paying only part of the amount today, type what they paid in Amount Received and the balance is shown.
6
Save and print. Press Ctrl+Enter, or click the big red Save & Print button. The bill is saved and the printer dialog opens.
Typing faster Press Enter inside the item table to jump down the same column, and a new line is added automatically when you reach the bottom. You never need the mouse to make a bill.

3. Printing, PDF and WhatsApp

Printing

Press Ctrl+Shift+P to open the Print Preview. The whole A4 page is shown exactly as it will print. At the bottom you choose the Printer and press Print — the bill goes straight to that printer at the correct A4 size.

Ctrl+P or Save & Print does the same in one step, sending the bill to your usual printer.

Why there is no Windows print window The preview inside the software is the print preview, and printing goes directly to the printer you pick. This is faster at the counter and avoids an extra window on every bill. If you ever need printer-specific options such as a paper tray or double-sided printing, click System dialog… in the preview to open the Windows print window instead.

The page size, orientation and margins are set by the software, so there is nothing to configure. Never turn on "Fit to page" in the Windows dialog — it shrinks the sheet and moves the weight columns out of line.

Saving a PDF

Press Ctrl+E, choose where to save, and the file is created. Your file manager opens with the PDF highlighted.

Sending on WhatsApp

Press Ctrl+W or click WhatsApp. What happens next depends on how the software has been set up:

  • Normal setup: WhatsApp opens on the customer's chat with the message already written, and the PDF is highlighted in your file manager. Drag the PDF into the chat and send.
  • Automatic setup: the PDF is sent to the customer by itself.

4. Finding and reprinting an old bill

Go to Invoices (Ctrl+3). You can:

  • Type a bill number, customer name or mobile number in the search box
  • Choose a date range — Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, or All
  • Filter by Cash, Cheque or Online

Each row has buttons: View to see it, Reprint to print another copy, PDF to save it, WhatsApp to send it, and Duplicate to start a new bill using the same items.

Reprints are marked A reprinted bill carries a small Duplicate Copy mark, so it is never mistaken for the original.

Correcting a mistake

A bill made today can be corrected: open Invoices, find it, and click Edit. Make the change and save again.

A bill from an earlier day cannot be changed, because it has already gone into the books. Instead click Cancel on it and make a fresh bill. A cancelled bill stays in the records for your GST return but stops counting as a sale.

5. Customers

Go to Customers (Ctrl+2). Search by name, mobile, GST number or PAN. Click any customer to see everything they have ever bought and how much they have spent in total.

Customers are saved automatically every time you bill someone, so this list builds itself.

6. Backup — please read

Your bills live on this computer. If the computer is lost, stolen or damaged, the bills go with it unless you have a backup. Doing this takes under a minute.

Every day: save a backup file

1
Go to Backup & Settings (Ctrl+5) and click Offline Backup.
2
Click Save Backup File.
3
Save it onto a pen drive, and keep the pen drive somewhere other than the shop.

You can also set this to happen by itself: choose a folder under Automatic Backup Folder and set On Closing the App to write a backup. Then a backup is written every time you close the software.

Restoring from a backup

Click Restore From File and choose your backup file. Bills already on the computer are not duplicated — the software matches them by bill number.

Cloud backup

If cloud backup has been set up for you, click Back Up Now under Cloud Backup to send everything to your online account. Restore From Cloud brings it all back.

7. Emergency Backup & Clear Device

This permanently erases all bills and customers from this computer Use it only if the computer may be seen by someone who should not see your records.

What the button does, in this order:

1
Sends every bill and customer to your online account.
2
Reads it all back and checks it arrived complete and correct.
3
Only then erases everything from this computer.

If the upload fails, or the check fails, nothing is deleted and the software tells you what went wrong. Your shop details stay, so the software still works normally afterwards.

To use it: Backup & Settings → Emergency → type BACKUP AND CLEAR exactly → confirm.

To get everything back later, use Restore From Cloud.

There is no undo Once cleared, the only copy is the one online. Make sure cloud backup has been tested and works before you ever use this button.

9. If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
The bill prints too small or columns do not line up In the printer window set scale to 100%, not "Fit to page", and paper to A4.
It says Offline Nothing is wrong. Billing, printing and saving all work. Only WhatsApp and cloud backup need internet.
A customer's details did not fill in Type at least two letters and wait a moment for the list to appear, then pick from the list rather than typing over it.
The bill number is wrong You can type over it in the Invoice No box before saving. If the number is already used by another bill, the software will tell you.
The shop name or GSTIN on the bill is wrong These are fixed in the software and cannot be changed from Settings, so a mistake at the counter can never reach a bill. Contact TridentCrew on 9096310817 to correct them.
I cannot edit an old bill Only today's bills can be edited. Cancel the old one and make a fresh bill.
The software will not start Restart the computer and try again. If it still fails, contact your supplier — do not reinstall, as that is not needed to fix it and your bills are safe on the computer.
Where your bills are stored All records are kept on this computer at
%APPDATA%\Prem Jewellers Billing\prem-jewellers.db
Never delete or move this file. To make a copy, use Save Backup File instead.
